PQR format as written by pdb2pqr with `--chain`: one `ATOM` line per atom, hydrogens
included, with the columns atom number, atom name, residue name, chain, residue number, x,
y, z, partial charge (e) and radius (Å). Atom and residue names follow the AMBER force
field (e.g. `HIE`, `HID`, `CYX`). The metal is absent.
