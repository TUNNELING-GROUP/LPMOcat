PDB format, one file per structure: the `ATOM` records of one chain, one conformer per
residue, standard residue names, and at most one `HETATM` record, the active-site metal.
Header records of a PDB-format download are kept. The B-factor column holds B-factors for
experimental structures and pLDDT for models; atoms rebuilt in 3a have occupancy 0.00 and
B-factor 0.00. Residue numbers are those of the source file.
